Drawer

Full-height side panel with header, scrolling content, and footer slots. Open it in the template with [(open)] or from ElDrawerService.open() with a custom component and injected data.

Add to your project: npx @ng-elemental/cli add drawer (requires icon; examples also need button)

Installation

Usage

Mark projected nodes with elDrawerHeader, elDrawerContent, or elDrawerFooter. Empty slots collapse. Use elDrawerClose on a button to dismiss. Focus is trapped while open; Escape and the backdrop dismiss unless disabled.

Open with a service

No extra host in the app shell. open() mounts the drawer on document.body and injects EL_DRAWER_DATA plus ElDrawerRef into the custom component.
