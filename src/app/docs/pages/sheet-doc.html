Sheet

Edge panel with header, scrolling content, and footer slots. Open it in the template with [(open)] or from ElSheetService.open() with a custom component and injected data.

Add to your project: npx @ng-elemental/cli add sheet (requires icon; examples also need button)

Preview

Open sheet
Header and footer stay put. Long content scrolls inside the panel.

Installation

Usage

Mark projected nodes with elSheetHeader, elSheetContent, or elSheetFooter. Empty slots collapse. Use elSheetClose on a button to dismiss.

Open with a service

No extra host in the app shell. open() mounts the sheet on document.body and injects EL_SHEET_DATA plus ElSheetRef into the custom component.
