Stepper

Tab-like step list that shows one ng-template at a time. Click any enabled step, or call next() / previous() from buttons you compose yourself.

Add to your project: npx @ng-elemental/cli add stepper (requires icon)

Usage

Import the stepper, step, and content directive. Put panel markup in <ng-template elStepContent> so inactive steps are not created. Optional elStepLabel templates replace the text label.
